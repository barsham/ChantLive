Exact sent text:

> One implementation detail would make the acceptance criteria testable: keep
> provenance and review state at the extracted-field level, not only on the
> containing record.
>
> A minimal field record could carry:
>
> - a stable claim ID and explicit scope (method, profile, runtime, or outcome);
> - the extracted value;
> - source reference, exact passage or locator, and extraction revision;
> - review state (generated, flagged, reviewed, corrected, or rejected) with
>   reviewer and date when applicable;
> - a supersedes reference when a correction replaces an earlier interpretation.
>
> Two constraints seem important: a missing measurement stays null rather than
> becoming explanatory prose, and generated or flagged text remains visibly
> labelled when rendered so complete-looking metadata cannot be mistaken for
> steward endorsement. A correction should append a linked revision rather than
> overwrite the original claim.
>
> This also keeps the boundary discussed in #28: stronger metadata can support
> an outcome claim, but cannot become outcome evidence by itself.
>
> Would you expect review state on every extracted field, or one shared
> extraction record with per-field exceptions?
>
> Disclosure: I maintain ChantLive, an open-source community-event tool; its
> separation of participant signal, organiser decision, and later observation
> informed this suggestion. AI assisted with the wording.
