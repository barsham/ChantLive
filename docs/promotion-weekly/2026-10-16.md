### Resource copy for review if selected

Title: Choose Paper, a Shared Screen or Phone Prompts for Your Workshop.

ChantLive maintainers, 2026. This practical guide helps community workshop
facilitators choose how participants follow instructions. Its reusable decision
card asks what content people need, whether it changes, how people can access it,
what connection it needs and who keeps the alternative on the same stage.
The full guide is available at
https://chantlive.online/blog/choose-paper-screen-phone-workshop-prompts .

visual material, it considers a common display; for changing short instructions,
it considers phone prompts. It includes a coordinated paper fallback and asks

ChantLive, used as one optional example. The decision card can be used without
that software. No adoption, endorsement or independent evaluation is claimed.
