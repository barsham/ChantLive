# ChantLive Organizer Office Hours

Status: Prepared for Barsham's review. Not published or scheduled.

## Purpose

Run one small, practical session where community organizers can test ChantLive
with a realistic but fictional event. This is not a sales demo, partnership
request, or endorsement exercise.

## Proposed Format

- Length: 20 minutes.
- Capacity: up to three community groups.
- Setup: each group uses two devices or two browser windows.
- Content: use harmless fictional event and chant text only.
- Cost: free; ChantLive is free and open source.

## Session Outline

1. Minutes 0-3: explain the organizer and participant roles and confirm that
   everyone will use fictional event content.
2. Minutes 3-10: run the seven-minute paired test from
   `docs/seven-minute-paired-test.md`.
3. Minutes 10-16: test late joining, closing and reopening the participant
   page, locking a phone, and recovering after an organizer mistake.
4. Minutes 16-20: collect three observations: where someone hesitated, the
   biggest trust or accessibility concern, and the single most useful change.
