# Community Rally Seven-Minute Test

Status: Prepared for testing. This is not a real event plan.

Use this scenario to test ChantLive without entering real participant
information, confidential material, or sensitive event content.

## Roles

- Organizer: creates the sample session and changes the current chant.
- Participant: joins from the ordinary public link and follows the updates.

One person may use two browsers or devices when a second tester is unavailable.

## Sample Session

Event name:

```text
Community Rally Test
```

Sample chants:

```text
Welcome everyone
Together for our community
Thank you for standing with us
```

These are harmless placeholders, not suggested messaging for a real event.

## Seven-Minute Run

1. The organizer creates the sample event and adds the three chants.
2. The organizer shares the ordinary participant link without relying on the
   QR code.
3. The participant joins after the organizer has already selected the first
   chant.
4. The organizer changes the current chant twice.
5. The participant locks and unlocks the device, or closes and reopens the
   participant page.
6. The organizer selects the final chant and checks whether the participant
   understands that the session has changed.

## Three Questions

Reply with short answers:

1. Where did the organizer or participant hesitate or need spoken help?
2. What is the biggest trust, accessibility, privacy, or reliability concern?
3. What one change would make the flow more useful at a community event?

Include the device, browser, or assistive technology when it affected the
result. A few sentences are enough.
