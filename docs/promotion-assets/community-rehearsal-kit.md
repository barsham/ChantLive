# Three-prompt community rehearsal kit

Prepared 19 September 2026 under Promotion Strategy V3. A reusable draft asset,
not a published offer, booked session, customer testimonial or completed test.

## Offer

We help a community organiser prepare three short prompts people can follow
on their phones, plus a printable fallback, before trying it at a small session.
The first rehearsal uses fictional or organiser-approved non-sensitive content.
No participant account is needed. Internet connectivity is required for updates.
This is a prompt display, not an emergency communication or audio timing system.

## Qualify before setup

Ask in the existing opted-in conversation:

- What short instructions or responses do people currently struggle to follow?
- How do you show them today, and would paper or a shared screen be simpler?
- Is there a session in the next month where a short rehearsal would help?
- Are phones welcome, and what alternative will people without phones use?

Do not ask for participant lists, political affiliations, private locations or
personal circumstances. An approximate session size and general format suffice.

## Draft for a permitted resource or demo slot

Title: Three readable prompts for a small community session

When the words change during a session, a static handout can leave people
looking at different instructions. Start with three short prompts, name who
changes them, and keep a printed copy with a helper. If a shared slide or paper
sheet already works, there may be no reason to add another tool.

I maintain ChantLive, a free open-source tool that displays live text prompts
on participants' phones. I am exploring whether a prepared rehearsal is useful
for small community gatherings: I can prepare three non-sensitive prompts and
a printable fallback for an organiser who wants to try this approach.

You would open a participant link, watch a prompt change, reopen the page, and
tell me whether it helps the session you actually run. Participants need no
account. Live updates require internet access; keep the paper version available.

What are you using now to keep people on the current instruction?

## Contextual response structure

Read the full available thread. Explain the practical answer to the person's
specific problem first, including a paper or shared-screen option. State the
ChantLive relationship before mentioning it. Offer a rehearsal only if it fits
and the community allows it. Do not pretend to be an independent customer.

## Ready-to-adapt sample prompts

| Stage | Leader text | Participant text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome | Welcome to the community workshop | You can listen, speak or pass |
| Reflection | Think of one thing that would help this session | Take a quiet moment; sharing is optional |
| Close | Thank you for taking part | Keep one useful idea for next time |

These are original sample words. They are not a new published ChantLive event.
Do not use copyrighted lyrics or private participant submissions without rights
and permission. The organiser approves any real-session wording before use.

## Deliver the rehearsal after opt-in

1. Create an isolated, clearly labelled fictional rehearsal using the existing
   organiser workflow. Keep it separate from the public demonstration and any
   real event. Avoid promised automation that has not been verified.
2. Add the three approved prompts. Prepare the participant link, optional QR
   code and printable handout. Verify both the link and fallback yourself.
3. Let the organiser experience a live change and reopen the page. If async,
   verify the existing rotation flow first; if synchronous, confirm availability.
4. Ask: Which prompt did you see after reopening? What was confusing? Would
   this improve your session compared with your current method, and why?
5. Record first-hand evidence, help needed and any failure. Maintainer checks
   are QA only. A reply saying "looks good" is not a completed rehearsal.
6. If useful, offer an organiser handover and a dated small pilot. Set up access
   through their own account. Do not share credentials or take over an actual
   event without its organiser's explicit agreement.

## Paper fallback

Print the approved three-row prompt table with the event's participant link.
The helper announces the current stage and points to its row. The printout is
static; it will not reflect edits made after printing. Check it at rehearsal
and before use. A phone or QR scan must not be the only participation path.
