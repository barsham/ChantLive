# ChantLive promotion strategy V3

Active from 19 September 2026, following Barsham's request to diagnose the
strategy and make radical changes. Supersedes V2's acquisition choices and
global waiting rules. Existing sending permissions, opt-outs, privacy and
release safeguards remain in force. All dates use Australia/Sydney.

## Diagnosis

The recorded baseline is 59 first contacts, four substantive external
interactions, one qualified test interest, zero confirmed completed tests and
zero confirmed pilots. These are ledger counts, not a fresh inbox audit.
Vanessa's follow-up was sent by Barsham on 19 September and verified in Zoho
Sent. Monitor for a reply or test observation and do not send another follow-up.

| Observed evidence | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold organisational requests produced no confirmed tests | That offer and recruitment process failed | That every organisation has no need for ChantLive |
| One DEV post had 15 recorded readers at an early snapshot and one conceptual question | Very limited demonstrated reach and unclear positioning | A reliable activation rate or a representative market test |
| A running demo in a GitHub issue received no recorded feedback | Removing setup alone did not produce a completed test | Whether nobody visited or people visited and left |
| A broken testing-community submission and an age-verification hold stalled work | Channel access was a dependency failure | Rejection of the product by customers |
| One interested tester froze unrelated recruitment | The operating rules created a bottleneck | A reason to abandon or pressure that tester |
| Blog releases continued without attributed trials | Content delivery worked | That search visibility or adoption improved |

The leading hypotheses are an offer with too little immediate benefit, weak
access to people with a current need, and slow experimental learning. They are
not proven causes. Product-market fit remains unknown. More features or broader
email volume do not resolve that uncertainty by themselves.

## Change the offer and audience

Lead with a useful result: **we will prepare a three-prompt, phone-readable
rehearsal for your next small community gathering.** Do the initial setup for
the organiser after they opt in. Give them a participant link, printable
fallback and a short handover. Their first task is judging whether the result
helps their own session, not testing an unfamiliar software product for us.

Initial audience: facilitators and volunteer organisers of recurring, small,
peaceful community workshops or gatherings who already need to show changing
short prompts without a shared screen. A 5-30 person session in the next month
is a screening hypothesis, not a capacity guarantee. Do not recruit people
based on political affiliation or sensitive personal characteristics.

Qualify the problem before proposing the app. Ask how they currently show the
words, what fails, and whether participants can or want to use phones. If a
printed sheet or shared slide solves it more simply, recommend that. Exclude
emergency communication, offline-only operation, full lyric/song libraries,
audio synchronisation, and requirements the current product cannot meet.

Use [the rehearsal kit](promotion-assets/community-rehearsal-kit.md). A live
call is optional; asynchronous preparation avoids making Barsham's calendar a
recruitment dependency. Never promise live staffing or dates without confirmed
availability. Participants do not need accounts; an organiser who later takes
control uses their own authorised account, never a shared password.

## Two independent work lanes

**Acquisition, owned by Promotion:** one active offer/audience hypothesis may
use up to two independently tracked, permitted public placements. Reply to an
existing relevant request or use an explicitly permitted resource/demo slot.
Every placement needs its own source, context, rules and outcome record. An
unanswered lead does not stop this lane.

**Support, owned by Outreach:** help people who opted in, monitor replies and
maintain one next action per person. Pending approval, silence or a technical
problem blocks only that person's next action. Vanessa's next action is reply
monitoring only. No new direct email or private-message permission is granted
here.

Do not duplicate replies across lanes. Check the contact ledger and other task
before acting. One person owns each conversation. If shared files are being
edited, write a dated companion ledger and link it from the active strategy.

## Acquisition experiment: prepared rehearsal

Initial window: September 21 through October 5, 2026.

1. Screen up to ten public opportunities across at most three candidate
   communities. Look for a recent request about readable live instructions,
   audience prompts or projector alternatives. A community homepage is not a
   qualified lead. Record the exact thread and stated problem.
2. Verify current rules and account access. Publish only when the exact action
   is covered by existing authorisation and those rules. Disclosure of our
   relationship is mandatory. No generic launch post, copied cross-post, cold
   private message, member harvesting, manufactured engagement or unsolicited
   email. A public contact address is not an invitation to a sales email.
3. Offer a complete useful answer in the channel itself. Introduce the prepared
   rehearsal only where it fits the stated problem. Use the kit's draft as a
   starting point, not a script to paste indiscriminately.
4. Aim for two qualified placements by September 25. Fewer is acceptable when
   there are no appropriate opportunities; record the failed access or supply
   assumption and change the route rather than manufacture activity.
5. After opt-in, prepare three approved non-sensitive prompts and the fallback.
   Ask the organiser to open the participant view, see a change, reopen it,
   and give one observation about their own session. Log assistance required.
6. After a useful rehearsal, ask whether they want to use it for one small
   session. A stated date is a commitment, not a completed pilot. After actual
   use, ask whether they would use it again and what they used instead before.

Targets, not forecasts: three qualified problem conversations, two completed
rehearsals and one dated pilot commitment by October 5. Aim for one completed
pilot and a repeat-use decision by October 19. These small counts provide
directional learning, not statistically reliable conversion estimates.

## Decisions and alternatives

- A platform access problem gets at most two business days and one reasonable
  recovery attempt. Then park that platform and evaluate a different permitted
  route. Never bypass a CAPTCHA, age check, fee, moderation or permission gate.
- September 23: review access and opportunity quality. No qualified opportunity
  means distribution is still untested; do not diagnose product rejection.
- September 28: compare placements, qualified replies, starts and completions.
  Visits unknown means unknown, never zero. Keep helping opted-in people.
- October 5: decide continue, change or stop. No relevant replies after two
  suitable placements means change the audience or offer, not more copies.
  Replies without rehearsals mean investigate effort/trust. Completed rehearsals
  without intent to use mean investigate usefulness versus the simpler alternative.
- Next route if contextual placements fail: an invited five-minute practical
  demonstration inside an existing facilitator/community technology session.
  Use an explicit speaker/resource submission invitation or opt-in introduction;
  organiser permission and attendance logistics must be verified first. A support
  address or general events listing is not permission to pitch.
- A reusable resource-library contribution is another bounded distribution
  option only where submissions are expressly accepted. Measure resulting
  qualified conversations, not backlinks. Do not upload a disguised advert.
- Paid research can test usability if Barsham separately approves a budget.
  Compensated participants are not organic demand or evidence of willingness to
  adopt. Do not buy ads or recruit paid testers under this plan.
- Do not pivot into choirs, religious communities or another segment merely
  because it is searchable. Require an expressed matching problem first.

## Measurement and content

Record separately: opportunity screened; placement accepted; qualified reply;
confirmed start; completed rehearsal plus observation; dated pilot commitment;
actual pilot; repeat use. Include date, source URL, assistance and evidence.
Report unique people within the experiment so repeat replies are not new leads.
Use self-reported discovery source or distinct placement links where supported;
do not add tracking pixels, fingerprinting or collect sensitive audience data.
Current viewer count is not cumulative visitors. No denominator means no rate.

Reserve about 60% of Promotion time for distribution and preparing opted-in
rehearsals, 25% for reviewing evidence and coordination, and 15% for content.
Keep the user-requested weekly blog, with one practical reusable asset and a
relevant next step. Prefer actual audience questions, use AnswerThePublic when
available, and disclose fallback research. Do not create extra SEO posts or
feature releases to compensate for missing adoption. Never claim indexing,
ranking, traffic or conversion without evidence.

Promotion runs Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 18:00. Only Monday includes the
weekly article. Outreach remains weekdays at 10:00. Enhancement remains Sunday
at 18:00 and prioritises reproducible blockers or accepted pilot requirements;
it need not invent feature work when no evidence supports it.

  restrict unwanted promotional messages. Use an invited session route only;
  do not prospect through member lists.
