## 1.1.85 - 16 August 2026

A restart-safe anonymous Q&A lifecycle that lets participants privately track questions while organisers spotlight, answer, moderate, and review them end to end.

### Added

- Durable anonymous questions and votes: Questions and one-vote-per-device signals now use shared event-scoped storage, stay idempotent across repeat taps, and survive reconnects, restarts, and multi-instance deployments without storing participant identities. [GitHub issue #13](https://github.com/barsham/ChantLive/issues/13)
- Private participant question receipts: Participants can see the status of questions submitted from their device, follow published answers, and withdraw their own still-open question through a deliberate two-step mobile control. [GitHub issue #13](https://github.com/barsham/ChantLive/issues/13)
- Live organiser Q&A desk: The active controller can spotlight one question, compose and publish an answer, dismiss inappropriate submissions, and recover loading or network failures from an accessible Command Center queue. [GitHub issue #13](https://github.com/barsham/ChantLive/issues/13)
- Multilingual live and published answers: Participant screens now announce the question being answered and its published response in English, Spanish, French, Arabic, and Persian, with socket updates and reload recovery. [GitHub issue #13](https://github.com/barsham/ChantLive/issues/13)
- Post-event Q&A outcomes report: Printable and copied event reports now retain question totals, votes, answer rate, moderation outcomes, and published responses while excluding device hashes and withdrawn content. [GitHub issue #13](https://github.com/barsham/ChantLive/issues/13)
