## 1.1.68 - 29 July 2026

More accessible participant logistics and faster organiser invitations.

### Improved

- Touch-friendly participant controls: Join fields, language controls, sharing actions, and connection-recovery controls now provide dependable 44px-or-larger tap targets.
- Complete participant sharing: Participant sharing now includes the selected-language event time, venue, meeting point, and arrival guidance alongside the link and code.
- One-tap event directions: Participants can open directions from waiting, live, and ended event-detail panels without retyping the venue or meeting point.
- Dashboard complete invitations: Each organiser event card can copy a ready-to-send invitation containing the participant link, short code, saved logistics, and no-account reassurance. [GitHub issue #15](https://github.com/barsham/ChantLive/issues/15)

### Fixed

- Keyboard-operable event cards: Organisers can focus a dashboard event card and open its setup with Enter or Space, with a clear focus indicator and accessible name.
