## 1.1.97 - 30 September 2026

A trust and privacy release shaped by a real external test: no remote fonts, reliable sharing, a clear policy, visible source links, and genuinely separable attendance identity.

### Fixed

- No remote font requests: Every ChantLive page now uses the device's system font stack instead of requesting Google Fonts at runtime. [Community feedback issue #13](https://github.com/barsham/ChantLive/issues/13)
- Visible event-sharing recovery: Participant and organiser sharing now reports success or cancellation and opens a selectable complete invitation when native sharing or clipboard access cannot finish. [Community feedback issue #13](https://github.com/barsham/ChantLive/issues/13)
- Attendance identity that is actually forgotten: Attendance now uses its own random per-event identifier. Forgetting deletes and discards it, re-enabling creates a new identity, and the UI explains that unrelated reservations, votes, questions, safety responses, and reports remain available. [Privacy details](/privacy#attendance)

### Added

- Plain-language privacy policy: A new accessible privacy page explains organiser accounts, participant hashes and content, browser storage, retention, third parties, and deletion limits. [Read the privacy policy](/privacy)

### Improved

- Privacy and source links in every core journey: Landing, sign-in, registration, authenticated dashboard, and participant screens now keep the privacy policy and open-source repository discoverable. [Source code](https://github.com/barsham/ChantLive)
